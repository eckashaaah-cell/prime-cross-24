The search included adjacent work on harmonic sums, logarithm sequences and zeta-zero oscillations. It did not establish that the two-prime specialization or its endpoint constants have never appeared elsewhere. The release makes no priority claim. The explicit modular representative is elementary, and the paper gives an equivalence concerning RH rather than an unconditional estimate for the eight-spoke sum.
